Object 01 / Metal

The key that opens nothing

Its lock disappeared years ago. The permission it represented did not.

This key was kept in the back of a sewing drawer, wrapped in a grocery receipt. No one remembers which door it opened. What remains is the habit of saving it — a small promise that the place might still exist somewhere.

A sewing drawer

Object 02 / Paper

One unused ticket

A journey can matter even when it never happens.

The date has faded, but the destination is still readable. The ticket was bought for a trip that was postponed once, then indefinitely. It became less a piece of transportation than a record of an imagined future.

Object 03 / Magnetic tape

A voice under the static

The recording is damaged. The pause before the first word is perfectly clear.

Side A begins with room noise, a chair scraping, and someone trying not to laugh. Most of the song has warped with time. The ordinary sounds around it are what make the tape impossible to throw away.

A box labeled “misc.”

Object 04 / Photograph

The person outside the frame

The photograph shows a place. The fingerprints show who carried it.

The landscape is carefully centered, but the back contains a hurried note addressed to someone who is never named. The image records a sunset; the worn corners record all the times it was shown to another person.

Found
Between two cabinet shelves
Value
One exposure / many retellings
